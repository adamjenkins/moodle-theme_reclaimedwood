# Changes

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-17)

- First release: a Boost child theme for Moodle 5.3 that brings back the Moodle 1.9 Wood theme's plywood look.
  Wood-grain page, panelled blocks and main region, plank navbar and page header, carved headings, the 1.9
  sliding-door tabs on the secondary navigation, course sections panelled as in 1.9 (pale panel with a
  plank down the side, activities on the panel), and the 1.9 switch icons on course section toggles.
- Activity descriptions, Boost's white cards (profile, course cards, timeline), report filters and grade tables
  panelled on the wood.
- The 1.9 default user picture, the smiley with sunglasses, for users with no picture (also drawn on Boost's
  initials badges).
- The 1.9 colours (#330000 ink, #990000 links, #FFCC99/#330000 bevels) and fonts (Arial, Verdana, Helvetica,
  bold headings). A varnished variant for Boost's dark colour mode.
